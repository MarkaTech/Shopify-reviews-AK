# ReviewMaster — video edit brief

**For:** Palmier Pro
**Source:** 8 screen recordings, no voiceover, no on-camera audio
**Deliverable:** one continuous video, target **3:45**, 1920×1080, 30fps, MP4 (H.264)
**Purpose:** Shopify App Store submission — the reviewer watches this to confirm the app does what the listing claims.

---

## Global rules

- **No voiceover anywhere.** Every line of narration in this document becomes an **on-screen subtitle**. Nothing is spoken.
- **Burn the subtitles in.** Do not rely on a sidecar track — the reviewer may watch with captions off.
- **No music, or a very quiet bed under 20% .** This is a functional demo, not a promo. If in doubt, silent.
- **Cut every loading state.** Spinners, blank panels, page transitions — remove them and join the cut. They are honest but they read as slowness.
- **No transitions between shots.** Hard cuts only. One optional exception: a 4-frame cross-dissolve between the five sub-scenes inside Shot 3, to signal "same story, next step".
- **Speed up idle mouse travel** to 1.5× where nothing is happening. Never speed up a moment a subtitle is explaining.
- **Blur or crop any real email address** wherever one appears, especially in the Shot 3 inbox scene.

---

## Subtitle style

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Position | Lower third, centred, ~12% up from the bottom edge |
| Font | Any clean sans (Inter, Helvetica Now, SF Pro). Semi-bold. |
| Size | ~42px at 1080p |
| Colour | White text |
| Background | Black bar or rounded box at 75–85% opacity — **not** a drop shadow alone. App UI is light, so plain white text will vanish. |
| Line length | Max 2 lines, max ~42 characters per line |
| Padding | Cue appears ~0.3s before the action it describes, clears ~0.5s after |

Minimum time on screen per cue is **2 seconds**. The timings below already respect this — if you re-cut, keep the rule.

## Must not appear in the final cut

- The operator portal at `/admin` — internal tooling, not part of the merchant product.
- Any real customer email address, in the inbox scene or anywhere else.
- Any browser chrome that shows other tabs, bookmarks, or a second store.
- Loading spinners and blank panels.

If any of these are in the raw footage, crop, blur, or cut around them.
